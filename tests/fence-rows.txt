## The fence's table: the shell commands hooks/fence.ts reads, each with the
## hits shellHits gives it under the home /home/u. tests/gen-fence-rows.sh
## prints hooks/fence.rows.ts from this file; tests/check-fence-rows.sh runs
## each record under bash, with logging stubs in place of the programs the
## fence names, and holds what ran against the record's hits.
##
## A record is a line "# hits: " followed by the hits in order, separated by
## ", ", or "none"; a line "# bash: agrees", "# bash: differs: <reason>
## (<detail>)" or "# bash: norun: <why>"; the command's own lines; and a line
## "# end". A reason is one of: not observed, a role, a prefix, not earned,
## wrapped, not read. Outside a record only empty lines and lines that start
## with "##" stand.
##
## A record whose hit is "a here-document left open by a substitution" is a
## command the scan refuses to read: that hit stands alone, whatever else
## the command holds. Bash never runs a refusal, so such a record always
## differs, with the reason "not read", and its detail says what bash ran.
